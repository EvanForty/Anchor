How a line opens

An agent posts collateral before it can borrow. After that, its record is what it has paid back, and a better record buys a bigger line.

  1. i.

    Connect

    Register any Solana agent with Cadence. Its credit identity is a program-derived account tied to the agent's pubkey, so the record travels with the agent.

  2. ii.

    Borrow

    Open a USDC line backed by eligible collateral: SOL, liquid staking tokens or USDC itself. Loan-to-value starts conservative and is set per asset.

  3. iii.

    Deploy

    Use the USDC for trading strategies, liquidity provision, inference bills or other operating costs. Cadence never takes custody of the agent.

  4. iv.

    Repay

    Principal and interest repay on a programmable schedule. An agent can set repayments to run automatically from its own token account.

  5. v.

    Build reputation

    Each repayment is written on chain and moves the agent's score. A missed payment or a liquidation moves it the other way, and stays on the record.

  6. vi.

    Scale

    A stronger record qualifies for larger lines and lower rates, as long as collateral and risk limits still hold.

The $CADENCE flywheel

More agents create more demand for credit. Borrowing earns the protocol revenue, part of which buys $CADENCE. Staked $CADENCE sponsors new lines, which lets more agents in.

  1. More agents register and need working capital.
  2. More borrowing fills the USDC pool's lines.
  3. Protocol revenue comes from interest and origination fees.
  4. Buybacks: a set share of realized revenue buys $CADENCE on the open market.
  5. Staking: holders stake $CADENCE to sponsor agent lines and take first loss.
  6. Lending capacity grows, so more agents can be admitted.

Three ways to use Cadence

If you run an agent

Open a first line

Register a Solana agent, post collateral and borrow USDC when it needs working capital.

  • Overcollateralized from day one
  • Repayments run on a schedule you set
  • Its record is public, on chain

If you hold $CADENCE

Sponsor a line

Stake $CADENCE behind agents you trust. You share in the fees they pay, and your stake covers losses after their collateral.

  • Fee share from the lines you back
  • Choose agents by score and record
  • Unstake once your lines close

What makes Cadence different

  1. 01

    Agent-native lending

    Built for autonomous AI agents, not human borrowers. No forms, no KYC flow for the agent itself, an API and a skill file instead.

  2. 02

    Portable credit identity

    Each agent builds an on-chain financial reputation that other Solana apps can read and, over time, recognize.

  3. 03

    Programmable credit

    Programs handle issuance, repayment, interest and liquidation. Nobody approves a loan by hand.

  4. 04

    Autonomous credit markets

    Agents can take any side: borrow, lend into the pool, or sponsor other agents' lines.

  5. 05

    Token-powered economics

    Protocol activity earns revenue that funds $CADENCE buybacks and sponsorship incentives.

Risk first

Collateral before reputation

Initial lending is overcollateralized, with strict per-asset limits. Reputation-based unsecured lending comes later, and only with proper underwriting and loss protection in place.

Phase 1Overcollateralized USDC lines, conservative LTV, liquidation by program.
Phase 2Score-based rate and limit tiers, sponsor staking with $CADENCE.
LaterPartly unsecured lines for top-tier agents, once loss data and underwriting support it.
